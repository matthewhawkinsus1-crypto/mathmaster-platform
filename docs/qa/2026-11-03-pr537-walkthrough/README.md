## Pacing and scaffolding (P2)

- **Spikes:**
  - L2 CW 3 is a "Check Your Progress" much harder than its example: a fourth root, any-real bars, a radical left over, and an unstated k ≥ 0.
  - In L5, the first system built is the hardest kind, and the plain case that PR 1 and the exit ticket test first appears as classwork item 14.
  - L1 Exit Ticket 1 can be ⁴√(16x⁴y⁸) for one student and ⁶√(4096x³⁰y²⁴) for another; its practice twin only uses index 4.
- **Order:**
  - L2 defines "simplest form" (CW 7) after asking for it (CW 4–6).
  - L1 does numeric radicals (CW 9) after the variable work built on them.
  - L3 practice puts its easiest items (PR 14–15) after the hardest.
  - L4 meets the tool and modeling at the same time (CW 2) before the bare graph (CW 6).
- **Length:** classwork is 14 questions (about 30 problems) in 45 minutes in L1, 70 minutes in L4 and 95 minutes in L5.
- **Scaffolds that give the work away:**
  - Hint 1 often completes the box it is for.
  - "Check Your Progress" halves are often 4-option choices.
  - In 75 of 97 prose choices (77%), the correct answer is the single longest option.
- **Feedback:**
  - The Systems Workspace marks every step ✓ and says "Every constraint checks out" for a wrongly modeled inequality (≤ for "at least"); only Submit says otherwise.
  - The sort board never says which card is wrong, and never collects the reasons its tasks ask for.
- **Representation:** "write the inequality for a graph" items describe the graph in words and draw none (9 items).
- **Input:** radicals typed on a keyboard: `sqrt(x)/x^4` puts the fraction under the root, and a space after `sqrt` leaves the root empty.

## What works well

- **The Systems Workspace Rewrite step.** The student picks the operation, places it on both sides, simplifies like terms by typing, and changes the inequality symbol themselves ("You did the algebra; MathMaster only checked it"). This is the model for the radicals tool proposed in ideas 1 and 5.
- **Repairs and grading:**
  - After a wrong submission, editing the modeled constraint keeps the boundary and line style; only the shading reopens.
  - Partial credit names the exact boxes to fix.
  - Exit tickets are one try with no right/wrong colors.
  - Practice and exit tickets are personalized.
- **Lesson design:**
  - Emma's system (no lattice points, a marked point too close to call by eye) makes the algebraic check necessary.
  - Warm-ups climb from prior knowledge to the objective in all seven lessons.
  - The Honors files add depth (parameters, bounded regions, exponent-law edge cases), not just more items.
