# Algebra I TEKS Fidelity V2 — Human Review Rubric

This rubric is for the second pass after the mechanical audit. It prevents a green structural report from being mistaken for instructional quality.

For every Algebra I TEKS standard, inspect all active student-facing families and classify the standard as KEEP, ENHANCE, or REBUILD.

## 1. Mathematical fidelity
- Does each family measure the actual TEKS, not merely nearby prerequisite or extension mathematics?
- Is every expected answer derived from the same parameters and information shown to the student?
- Can any legal generator draw create ambiguity, impossible values, duplicate answers, or a trivialized task?

## 2. Representation progression
Use representations only when mathematically appropriate. Do not invent a graph merely to satisfy a quota.
- symbolic/equation
- table
- graph/coordinate plane
- ordered pairs/mapping
- verbal/context
- interactive tool/model

A strong standard should support a coherent movement among representations where the TEKS calls for it.

## 3. Thinking progression
DOK and difficulty are different axes.
- DOK 1: recall/use a known procedure
- DOK 2: select/interpret/connect
- DOK 3: justify, reverse reason, analyze error, compare strategies, transfer
- Difficulty: structural complexity, arithmetic burden, number form, distractor closeness, amount of information, or representation complexity

Do not call larger numbers higher DOK.

## 4. Family durability
Five documents are not enough if they are five fixed questions or five skins of one task.
- parameter ranges must produce meaningfully different instances
- contexts should vary when context is instructional rather than decorative
- students should not learn a positional, sign, magnitude, or wording shortcut
- repeated sessions should surface different mathematical reasoning, not just different numbers

## 5. Misconception quality
For multiple choice or diagnostic tasks:
- wrong answers should come from authentic student errors
- avoid key±1/key±2/key±3 construction unless those values genuinely model misconceptions
- correct choice must not be identifiable by size, sign, length, format, or option position

## 6. Feedback and review
- feedback must address the likely mathematical error, not generic encouragement
- hints should move one conceptual step without giving away the answer
- solution review should explain why, not merely repeat arithmetic
- repeated boilerplate across most families is an ENHANCE signal

## 7. Connected learning sequence
Where a standard sits inside a broader concept, verify that its families connect to adjacent standards rather than functioning as isolated trivia.

Example for sequences:
term number/input -> table -> ordered pairs -> discrete graph -> recursive rule -> explicit rule -> interpretation/application

## Verdicts
### KEEP
The current families are mathematically sound, durable, varied, and instructionally coherent. Small wording edits do not change this verdict.

### ENHANCE
The core families are worth preserving, but the standard needs one or more of: stronger generator ranges, additional representation, better progression, better misconceptions, less generic feedback, higher-DOK family, or a richer tool-backed family.

### REBUILD
The majority of the families are invalid, clone-like, fragile, answer-leaking, badly aligned, or incapable of supporting repeated student use. Replace rather than patch.

## Release rule
No TEKS Fidelity V2 content is merged to main solely because the audit script is green. Human review and the existing full platform/V5/Path test gates must both pass.
